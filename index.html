What I do for you: three stages

01

Procure

Find the right CPO.

I identify and shortlist the best-fit Charging Point Operators for your asset — assessing financial strength, technology, pricing models and track record. You receive a clear recommendation with no conflicts of interest.

02

Negotiate

Lease & licence agreements.

I negotiate the licence on your behalf — securing favourable revenue sharing, protection of landlord rights, appropriate make-good provisions, and terms that hold up long-term. You don't sign blind.

03

Deliver

Construction & installation.

I oversee the full delivery process — design approvals, grid connection, civil works and commissioning — holding the CPO accountable to programme and quality. Your asset and tenants are protected throughout.

Why use an independent advisor?

  • Simplify the complex process of installing EV charging with genuinely independent advice tailored to your site.

  • Maximise the financial return of your car park by selecting the best long-term infrastructure partner.

  • EV charging is infrastructure — grid capacity, switchboard upgrades, easements and make-good all matter. Property expertise is essential, not optional.

  • A fixed advisory fee structure — you know the cost upfront, with no incentive to favour one operator over another.

  • Available to institutional owners, REITs, private landlords and strata-titled commercial assets across Australia.

PropEV is an independent advisory practice. I hold no financial interest in any charging operator and receive no commissions from hardware or software providers. All advice is given solely in the interests of the property owner.
